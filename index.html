About us

A Lichfield business, built one clean car at a time.

Crystal Car Cleaners started with a simple idea: bring a proper, professional valet to your door — no queuing at a jet wash, no giving up your Saturday morning.

Why we started

We were tired of the trade-off: a quick jet wash that misses the interior, or a full detailer that costs more than the car's worth and takes half your weekend. Crystal Car Cleaners closes that gap for people in Lichfield.

How we work

Every visit is carried out on-site with our own water, power and eco-friendly products. We book in tight local rounds around Lichfield, which keeps prices down and means we can usually get to you within the week.

Reliability

If we say we'll be there, we're there. Turning up on time is the whole job.

Care for your car

We treat every car like it's about to be judged by its owner — because it is.

Fair pricing

Clear, upfront prices with no surprise add-ons at the end of the job.
